## Summary

Phase 1.1.1 achieves its stated goal in full. Every must-have is implemented correctly and covered by tests:

- The processor confirmation endpoint is tri-state with no false-approve path — `classifyNuveiStatus` is strict and pending is the safe default.

- DMN deduplication is PPP-based — fiscal ID comparison is removed.
- The enabler correctly interprets 402 as a business outcome and routes all three outcomes to distinct callbacks with no cross-contamination.
- Card and drop-in confirm with the known processor session token, not a browser-side value.
- No polling is present.
- Both route paths (`/payment-intents/:id`, `/webhooks/nuvei`) are unchanged.
- All documentation reflects the implemented contract.

128 processor tests and 61 enabler tests all pass.
